Selected work

Prussian Motors, BigCommerce to Shopify

2026

A freelance rebuild I owned start to finish in four months. I moved the store to a Shopify Hydrogen storefront (React Router, TypeScript, Oxygen) with preview deploys for every pull request, and updated the company’s Next.js and NestJS admin to run the business on Shopify: it lists and removes products as inventory changes, manages eBay listings and scheduled markdowns, and logs every job. Then I ran the cutover: order history imported into Shopify customer accounts, old URLs redirected to protect search rankings, and carrier-calculated shipping and local pickup set up.

Price Alerts

2026

Built end to end: alert storage, fare-drop search queries, daily price checks, signup on ship and itinerary pages, the /price-alerts page, emails through Braze and a dashboard in Bridge. It launched in the US, UK and Australia in September 2026 and drew 3,200+ alerts from 2,300+ people, and 900+ new email subscribers, in its first seven weeks. It’s a modern take on the price-drop alerts I first shipped on the PHP platform in 2016.

Rebuilding Find a Cruise

2025

Find a Cruise is the cruise search page and Cruise Critic’s largest source of revenue. I rebuilt it on React Server Components and rolled it out with structured data, indexing rules and revenue tracking in place from day one, then went on to own the wider App Router migration. Years earlier I’d built the Elasticsearch-backed API behind it, with faceted deal search and score-based ranking, and later cut the page down to a single API call with full-page caching in front of it.

Bridge, the internal admin app

2024–2026

Much of Bridge is mine: SEO templates for ship, destination and deal pages, port management, and sponsored-deal and ad-request tools. I also moved the remarketing feeds for Google, Bing, Facebook, Taboola and TikTok off cron onto Inngest and Convex, with run history, charts, alerting and drop validation.

The serverless GraphQL platform

2020–2023

When Cruise Critic rebuilt on Next.js, I built and owned core services on its GraphQL platform, Apollo Federation on AWS Lambda: the main data subgraph, email sponsorship, partners, reviews and analytics. On top of it I built the Partner Portal Deals backend, with its own database, JWT-authenticated GraphQL, click tracking and daily partner reports.

Moving email from ExactTarget to Braze

2023

I built IMS, the identity and subscription service, on WunderGraph and OpenSearch and published it as an npm SDK. Then I imported users from ExactTarget, SSO and Auth0, and removed the old ExactTarget code once nothing depended on it.

Pricing, tracking and partner billing

2014–2026

I built cruise price ingestion and ran it for over a decade: more than 50 of the site’s partner pricing parsers across FTP, S3, HTTP and email feeds, with change detection and alerting. I built the click and conversion pixel, the revenue reporting behind it and the CPC billing for meta partners, with vendor budgets and time-zone-aware daily caps. I also built the first meta pricing feeds and onboarded 23 partners and vendors, including Virgin Voyages, Viking and Hurtigruten.

Guardrails for AI coding agents

I’ve used Claude Code daily since 2025. I co-wrote our agent instructions and the policy that keeps agents from deploying, publishing or migrating production, enforced by a hook in each agent, so the team could use them freely without risking the live site.

How I work

I do my best work owning a hard problem from the first query to the last alert: a revenue-critical page, a migration nobody wants to touch, a pipeline that has to be right every night. I like working closely with a strong lead, co-leading projects and helping teammates along the way.

I care about leaving systems easier to change than I found them, which is why so much of my work has been upgrades: PHP 7 to 8.5 on the main platform, Next.js from the Pages Router to the App Router, Jenkins to GitHub Actions, hand-managed AWS to audited Terraform.
